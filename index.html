    Home Planner

    A furniture-layout tool built in Three.js. Real floor plans (traced from brochure images) and furniture models (built to scale from photos) are placed in 3D, with live collision and clearance checks as you drag things around.

    Highlights:

    • Parametric furniture generated in code — not imported models — each piece built from a few numbers (width/depth/height).
    • Floor plans extracted from brochure images with a small Python pipeline: wall colours and hand-traced lines become real geometry.
    • Separating-axis collision detection flags overlaps and blocked clearance (legroom, door swing, vent gaps) live, in both 3D and top-down view.
    • Multi-floor layouts shown side by side, with autosave and JSON import/export.

    Try the "Open hall" layout to drop furniture freely, or pick one of the floor plans and hit "Add all my items".
